# Competition Rules and Guidelines

## Source
Official “Competition Rules & Guidelines” PDF provided by the organizers.
This document records its contents; it does not add permissions that the PDF does not state.

## Confirmed rules

### Entry and equipment
- Lab entry is from 9:15 AM to 9:35 AM. No entry is allowed before or after that window.
- Bring a laptop, mouse, headphones, extension cords, and any other needed accessories.
- University computers are only available to participants who requested one in advance.

### Venue and breaks
- At least one team member must remain at the assigned spot; the whole team may not leave together.
- Participants are responsible for their belongings and equipment.
- Follow the organizers’ announced break schedule. Break times have not yet been finalized.
- Food and drinks are not allowed inside the labs. Personal water is allowed.

### Development and submission
- Development time is 6 hours and 45 minutes.
- Each team must upload its project and provide a live link for judging.
- No code updates or modifications are allowed after 4:00 PM.
- Presentations begin after the development period; presentation slots will be shared later.
- The judges’ decision is final.

## Clarifications required before relying on these rules

The PDF does not specify:
- Whether Claude Code or other AI tools may be used.
- Whether AI-generated code is permitted.
- Whether Stitch or other external AI design tools may be used.
- Whether internet access, online documentation, external APIs, or component libraries are allowed.
- Whether starter repositories, templates, or prebuilt code may be used.
- The required programming languages/frameworks or any prohibited technologies.
- The exact development start time and how it relates to the 4:00 PM code cutoff.
- The upload method and any required repository, branch, or submission format.

Ask the organizers for clarification on these points. In particular, confirm the development start time: a 6-hour-45-minute window starting at 9:35 AM would end after the stated 4:00 PM code cutoff.

## Working rule
Until a clarification is received, treat every item above as unresolved. Do not assume a tool, service, template, or coding approach is allowed.
