## Visual system
- Palette: define semantic colors for canvas, surface, text, primary action, accent, success, warning, and error.
- Typography: choose a distinctive but readable heading/body pairing. Use bundled/local fonts or safe fallbacks so the design works without network access.
- Layout: define content width, grid, navigation, spacing rhythm, and mobile behavior.
- Surfaces: specify borders, radii, elevation, and contrast. Avoid stacking cards just to fill space.
- Signature detail: one memorable visual element tied to the product or data.
- Motion: subtle and purposeful; respect reduced motion. 3D is optional and must not delay core flows.

## Quality bar
- Clear hierarchy; first screen immediately explains what matters.
- Real content density appropriate to the task; no giant empty hero on an operational app.
- Every control works or is clearly disabled/unavailable.
- Designed loading, error, empty, success, and validation states.
- Mobile layout is intentional, readable, and has no horizontal overflow.
- Keyboard focus and accessible names are visible.
- Do not use fake metrics in production-like views; label demo data.
- Avoid default AI tropes: purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, random blobs, excessive icons, generic dashboard cards, and meaningless animations.
